Gate 1

Eligible data breach

A breach is eligible under s26WE if it involves unauthorised access to, unauthorised disclosure of, or loss of personal information held by the organisation.

Did the incident involve personal information held by your organisation?

Personal information = any info about an identified or reasonably identifiable individual

Was there unauthorised access to, unauthorised disclosure of, or loss of that information?

Loss = losing physical control, e.g. stolen device, misdelivered file, exfiltration

Gate 2 next
Gate 2

Likely serious harm

Would a reasonable person in the organisation's position conclude the breach is likely to result in serious harm to one or more affected individuals? Answer each factor below.

Does the information include sensitive data?

Health, financial, government ID, biometric, sexual orientation, criminal record, etc.

Did the breach affect more than 50 individuals?

Was the breach the result of a malicious or criminal act?

e.g. ransomware, hacking, insider theft, social engineering

Could the information be reasonably used to commit identity fraud?

Combinations like name + DOB + ID number are particularly high risk

Could the information reasonably cause financial harm to affected individuals?

e.g. account takeover, Medicare fraud, credit fraud

Could the disclosure put any individual at risk of physical harm or harassment?

e.g. location data for a domestic violence victim, address + identity of a protected person

Gate 3

Exemptions

Even if Gates 1 and 2 are satisfied, notification may not be required if an exemption applies under Part IIIC of the Privacy Act.

Has the organisation taken remedial action that removes the likelihood of serious harm before individuals are affected?

e.g. encrypted data where the key was never compromised, or a misdirected email retrieved unread — s26WF exemption

Has a law enforcement body requested that notification be delayed or not made?

s26WN exemption — must be a formal request from an authorised body

Does another Australian law prohibit the notification?

e.g. certain intelligence legislation or court-ordered suppression
